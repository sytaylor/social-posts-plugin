# The 3-Filter Story Evaluation System

**Purpose:** Universal framework for evaluating which news/stories are worth posting about on LinkedIn.

**Principle:** Not all news is post-worthy. Filter ruthlessly to maintain quality and audience trust.

---

## Overview

Every potential story must pass through 3 filters before posting:

```
Story Candidate
    ↓
Filter 1: VOICE FIT
(Can you write this authentically?)
    ↓ PASS
Filter 2: AUDIENCE RELEVANCE
(Will your audience care?)
    ↓ PASS
Filter 3: PERFORMANCE POTENTIAL
(Will it get engagement?)
    ↓ PASS
Priority 1: Publish Today
```

**Fail 1 filter** = Reconsider or skip
**Fail 2+ filters** = Definitely skip

---

## Filter 1: The Voice Fit Filter

**Question:** Can I write about this authentically in my voice?

### ✅ PASS Criteria

**Story enables your authentic style:**
- Allows for your signature phrases/patterns
- Fits your analytical approach
- Doesn't require fake expertise
- Enables your unique perspective
- Doesn't force a tone you don't use

### ❌ FAIL Criteria

**Story requires inauthentic writing:**
- Demands hot take without nuance (if you're analytical)
- Only surface-level angles (if you go deep)
- Requires expertise you don't have
- Forces certainty where you have uncertainty
- Doesn't fit your content pillars

### Examples by Voice Type

**Analytical Voice:**
- ✅ "Company X invested $Y - here's why this matters for [infrastructure/second-order effects]"
- ✅ "I'm genuinely puzzled by this move. Here's what I think might be happening..."
- ❌ "This is INSANE! You NEED to pay attention!" (requires hype you don't use)
- ❌ "The 5 reasons why [trend] will change everything!" (forced listicle)

**Conversational Voice:**
- ✅ "This is wild. Let me explain what's happening..."
- ✅ "Okay, this is actually fascinating. Here's the deal..."
- ❌ "Per our analysis of Q4 FY25 data, we observe..." (too formal)
- ❌ Technical jargon without explanation (not conversational)

**Thought Leadership Voice:**
- ✅ "After 10 years in this industry, here's what I'm seeing..."
- ✅ "This validates what I've been saying for months..."
- ❌ Breaking news tweet-style (too reactive)
- ❌ Surface-level trend chasing (not thoughtful)

## Filter 2: The Audience Relevance Filter

**Question:** Will my target audience actually care about this?

### ✅ PASS Criteria

**Story provides value to YOUR audience:**
- Directly impacts their work/industry
- Helps them make decisions
- Gives them conversation starters
- Teaches them something useful
- Entertains while being relevant

### ❌ FAIL Criteria

**Story is irrelevant to YOUR audience:**
- Too niche (only 100 people care)
- Too broad (everyone already covered it)
- Wrong industry/vertical
- No actionable takeaway
- Pure entertainment with no relevance

### Audience Fit Test

Ask yourself:

1. **Would my ideal customer/reader forward this to a colleague?**
   - Yes = PASS
   - No = FAIL

2. **Does this help them do their job better or understand their industry?**
   - Yes = PASS
   - No = FAIL

3. **Is this the kind of content they'd expect from me?**
   - Yes = PASS
   - No = FAIL

### Examples by Audience Type

**FinTech Professionals:**
- ✅ Payment infrastructure changes
- ✅ Banking regulation updates
- ✅ Crypto institutional adoption
- ✅ AI in financial services
- ❌ Pure consumer app features (no B2B angle)
- ❌ Unrelated tech news (unless clear fintech connection)

**Startup Founders:**
- ✅ Fundraising trends
- ✅ Go-to-market strategies
- ✅ Scaling challenges/solutions
- ✅ Founder mental health
- ❌ Corporate politics at big companies
- ❌ Executive MBA admissions

**Product Managers:**
- ✅ Product strategy frameworks
- ✅ User research insights
- ✅ Roadmap prioritization
- ✅ Cross-functional leadership
- ❌ Engineering implementation details (too technical)
- ❌ Sales commission structures (wrong function)

---

## Filter 3: The Performance Potential Filter

**Question:** Will this actually perform well on the platform?

### ✅ PASS Criteria

**Story has engagement potential:**
- Clear "wait, what?" moment
- Specific details (names, numbers, amounts)
- Visual potential (can show not just tell)
- Timely (fresh, not old news)
- Unexpected angle or contrarian take

### ❌ FAIL Criteria

**Story lacks engagement triggers:**
- No hook (boring headline)
- Too abstract (no concrete details)
- No visuals possible
- Old news (>48h unless "Finally" angle)
- Same angle everyone else has

### High-Performance Indicators

**Specific Numbers:**
- ✅ "$200M investment"
- ✅ "629,000 followers"
- ✅ "3 examples in 6 months"
- ❌ "Big investment"
- ❌ "Lots of followers"
- ❌ "Recently"

**Name Brands:**
- ✅ Google, Stripe, OpenAI, MrBeast
- ✅ Well-known executives (Elon, Sam Altman)
- ❌ Unknown startups (unless unique angle)
- ❌ Generic "a company"

**Unexpected Combinations:**
- ✅ Crypto + MrBeast
- ✅ Banks + AI agents
- ✅ Traditional finance + Web3
- ❌ Expected pairings (VC invests in startup)
- ❌ Obvious trends (AI company uses AI)

**Visual Potential:**
- ✅ Celebrity photo available
- ✅ Before/after screenshots possible
- ✅ Data for chart creation
- ❌ Abstract concept only
- ❌ No visual elements

## Priority Matrix

Based on filter results, assign priority:

### Priority 1: PUBLISH TODAY ✅

**Criteria:** Passes ALL 3 filters + timely

**Characteristics:**
- Perfect voice fit
- Audience will love it
- Strong engagement potential
- Breaking news or fresh angle

**Action:** Create post ASAP

---

### Priority 2: DEVELOP THIS WEEK ⚠️

**Criteria:** Passes 2/3 filters, needs work

**Characteristics:**
- Good story, needs unique angle
- Requires more research for specifics
- Part of emerging pattern (wait for 3rd example)
- Could be "Finally" moment with context

**Action:**
- Research missing details
- Find unique angle
- Wait for pattern validation
- Connect to broader trend

---

### Priority 3: WATCHLIST 👀

**Criteria:** Interesting but not ready

**Characteristics:**
- First or second signal of pattern (need 3rd)
- Intriguing person/company to monitor
- Thesis developing (not validated yet)
- Too early stage (seed round, needs proof)

**Action:**
- Add to watchlist document
- Set Google Alert
- Check back in 30/60/90 days

---

### Priority 4: SKIP ❌

**Criteria:** Fails 2+ filters

**Characteristics:**
- Pure hype, no substance
- Too niche or too broad
- Wrong audience
- Old news, no new angle
- Generic/expected

**Action:** Skip entirely, move on

## Advanced Filtering Techniques

### The "3 Examples = Trend" Rule

**Don't post on first example. Wait for pattern.**

**Process:**
```
Example 1: Note in watchlist
→ "Banks enabling AI agents?"

Example 2: Add to Priority 2
→ "Pattern forming - research more"

Example 3: Move to Priority 1
→ "Banks are FINALLY building for AI agents"
```

**Post structure for trends:**
```
[Industry] is finally [doing X].

Company A → [Action] ([Date])
Company B → [Action] ([Date])
Company C → [Action] ([Date])

Maybe this is about [bigger insight].
```

---

### The "Both Sides" Test

**If you can ONLY see one side, it might not be a good story.**

**Good (enables both-sides):**
- "Maybe crypto is growing up. Or it's 2026 weirdness."
- "Could be institutional adoption. Could be desperation."
- "Validation of thesis OR outlier data point."

**Bad (only one angle):**
- "This is DEFINITELY the future!"
- "Everyone who disagrees is wrong!"
- "The only way to interpret this is..."

**Why it matters:** Both-sides thinking = thoughtful, not reactionary

---

### The "Would I Forward This?" Test

**Final gut check before posting:**

1. Would I send this to a smart colleague?
   - Yes = Probably worth posting
   - No = Probably skip

2. If they forwarded it to me, would I be glad?
   - Yes = Audience relevance confirmed
   - No = Wrong audience fit

3. Would I click if someone else posted this?
   - Yes = Performance potential good
   - No = Boring headline

## Common Filtering Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake 1: Chasing Every Trend

**Problem:** Posting on every viral topic

**Why it fails:** Loses your unique voice, becomes noise

**Fix:** Only post if you have unique angle that fits your voice

---

### ❌ Mistake 2: Too Inside Baseball

**Problem:** Stories only 100 people care about

**Why it fails:** No engagement, wastes strong hook

**Fix:** Apply Audience Filter rigorously

---

### ❌ Mistake 3: Too Late to the Party

**Problem:** Posting 3 days after everyone else

**Why it fails:** Unless "Finally" angle, you're noise

**Fix:** Post within 24-48h OR wait for pattern (3 examples)

---

### ❌ Mistake 4: Forcing Posts for Consistency

**Problem:** "I should post today" → posts weak story

**Why it fails:** Trains audience to expect low quality

**Fix:** Quality > consistency. Skip days when no P1 story.

---

### ❌ Mistake 5: Ignoring Voice Fit

**Problem:** "Everyone's talking about it, so I should too"

**Why it fails:** Inauthentic posts perform poorly

**Fix:** If it doesn't fit your voice, don't force it

## Filter Calibration

**Track over time to refine your filters:**

### Weekly Review

**For each Priority 1 story you posted:**
- Did it perform as expected? (engagement)
- Was voice authentic? (comments reflect your brand?)
- Did audience engage meaningfully? (discussion vs "great!")

**For each Priority 1 story you skipped:**
- Was that the right call?
- Did someone else crush it with that story?
- Could you have had unique angle?

### Monthly Calibration

**Tighten or loosen each filter:**

**Voice Filter:**
- Are you rejecting too many good stories? (too strict)
- Are you posting things that feel off-brand? (too loose)

**Audience Filter:**
- Are you getting engagement from the right people? (calibrated)
- Are you getting generic engagement? (too broad)
- Are you getting crickets? (too niche or wrong audience)

**Performance Filter:**
- Are your "high potential" stories actually performing? (accurate)
- Are you missing sleeper hits? (too conservative)

## Workflow Integration

### Morning Routine

```
1. Scan news sources (10 min)
   ↓
2. Apply Filter 1: Voice Fit
   → Discard non-fits immediately
   ↓
3. Apply Filter 2: Audience Relevance
   → Keep only audience-relevant stories
   ↓
4. Apply Filter 3: Performance Potential
   → Rank remaining by engagement likelihood
   ↓
5. Assign Priorities
   → P1 (publish), P2 (develop), P3 (watch), P4 (skip)
```

**Time:** 15-20 minutes total for full filtering

- Complexity matches your typical depth
- Tone requirements fit your style

- Gut feel on authenticity
- Specific angle you want to take

---

### Filter 2: Audience Relevance (90% automatable)

**AI can check:**
- Industry/topic tags match audience
- Company names in your target sectors
- Keywords match audience interests

**Human must check:**
- Subtle relevance (second-order effects)
- Strategic importance

---

### Filter 3: Performance Potential (70% automatable)

**AI can check:**
- Presence of specific numbers
- Name brand recognition
- Timeliness (publish date)
- Visual availability

**Human must check:**
- "Wait, what?" factor (gut feel)
- Contrarian angle potential
- Your unique take

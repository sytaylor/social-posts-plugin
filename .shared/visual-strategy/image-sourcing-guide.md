# Universal Visual Sourcing & Strategy Guide

**Purpose:** Find and create professional visuals for social media posts across any industry.

**Critical Rule:** Recognizability > Company Hierarchy > Abstract Concepts

---

## The Recognizability Rule

**ALWAYS prioritize the most recognizable/visual element:**

✅ **Celebrity/Well-known Person > Unknown Company**
- MrBeast (300M subscribers) > BitMine (unknown company)
- Sam Altman (household name) > Obscure AI startup
- Elon Musk > Any Tesla executive

✅ **Visual Product > Abstract Concept**
- Tesla Cybertruck photo > Tesla logo
- ChatGPT interface > OpenAI logo
- iPhone product shot > Apple logo

✅ **Person > Logo When Person IS the Story**
- Founder profile → Their face
- "This is [Name]" → Their headshot
- Executive move → Their photo

### Decision Tree
```
1. Is there a celebrity/well-known person? → Use their image
2. Is there a visual product/interface? → Use screenshot
3. Is it about a company move? → Use logo + context
4. Is it a dollar amount story? → Use logos + number
```

## Image Sourcing Strategies

### For People (Headshots, Celebrity Photos)

**Best Sources (in order):**

1. **Google Image Search**
   ```
   "[Person Name] headshot"
   "[Person Name] professional photo"
   "[Person Name] [Company Name]"
   "[Person Name] linkedin"
   ```
   Filter: Tools → Size → Large
   Filter: Tools → Usage rights → Creative Commons (if needed)

2. **LinkedIn Profile**
   - Visit their profile
   - Right-click profile photo → Save image
   - Usually lower res, but acceptable

3. **Company Press Kit**
   - `[company].com/press`
   - `[company].com/about/team`
   - `[company].com/newsroom`
   - Often includes high-res executive photos

4. **Crunchbase Profile**
   - Often higher quality than LinkedIn
   - Good for startup founders

5. **Wikipedia/Official Websites**
   - For public figures
   - Usually high quality, properly attributed

**Quality Check:**
- Minimum 800x800px
- Professional background
- Recent photo (check date)
- Clear, in-focus face

---

### For Company Logos

**Best Sources:**

1. **Clearbit Logo API** (Instant, Free)
   ```
   https://logo.clearbit.com/[domain].com

   Examples:
   https://logo.clearbit.com/stripe.com
   https://logo.clearbit.com/coinbase.com
   https://logo.clearbit.com/google.com
   ```
   - Returns high-res PNG
   - Transparent background
   - Works for any domain

2. **Company Press Kit**
   ```
   [company].com/press
   [company].com/brand
   [company].com/media
   ```
   - Look for "Brand Assets" or "Media Kit"
   - Often includes SVG (scalable) logos
   - Multiple formats available

3. **Crunchbase Company Page**
   - High-res company logos
   - Free to access
   - Good for startups

4. **Wikipedia Commons**
   - For major companies
   - Usually SVG format
   - Properly attributed

**File Format Preference:**
1. SVG (scalable, best)
2. PNG with transparency (good)
3. High-res JPG (acceptable)

---

### For Product Screenshots

**Methods:**

1. **Take Your Own** (Preferred)
   - Visit product website/app
   - Sign up for free trial if needed
   - Screenshot key features
   - Crop to show specific functionality

2. **Official Product Pages**
   - `[company].com/product`
   - `[company].com/features`
   - Company blog posts often embed screens

3. **YouTube Product Demos**
   - Official company channel
   - Pause at clearest frame
   - Screenshot (ensure high quality)

4. **Company Blog Posts**
   - Announcement posts
   - Feature updates
   - Usually include embedded screenshots

**Screenshot Best Practices:**
- Use largest resolution possible
- Crop tightly to relevant area
- Ensure text is readable
- Check for any personal/sensitive info

---

### For Data/Charts

**Never steal charts - Recreate them.**

**Data Sources:**
1. Company investor decks (if public)
2. Research reports (CB Insights, Gartner)
3. Press releases (numeric data)
4. Financial statements (public companies)

**Chart Creation Tools:**
- **Datawrapper** (easiest, free)
- **Flourish** (interactive, free tier)
- **Canva Charts** (simple, designed)
- **Google Sheets** → Chart → Export

**Quick Wins:**
- Bar charts (comparing deals, amounts)
- Line graphs (growth over time)
- Pie charts (market share)
- Icon grids (visual counts)

## Visual Format Library

### Format 1: Quote Over Face

**When:** Person-focused posts, celebrity stories

**Structure:**
```
[Professional headshot - 60% of image]
[Quote overlay - large, readable font]
[Attribution: Name, Title, Company - bottom]
```

**Tools:** Canva (use quote template)

**Example:**
```
MrBeast photo (large)
"$200M Investment in Creator IP"
MrBeast • Creator • 300M+ subscribers
```

---

### Format 2: Before/After Comparison

**When:** "Finally" moments, product evolution

**Structure:**
```
Left: "Before" or "Standard"
Right: "After" or "New"
Center: Clear dividing line or "vs."
```

**Tools:** Canva (side-by-side template), Figma

**Example:**
```
Left: Standard Chat screenshot
Right: Personal Intelligence screenshot
Labels: Clear headers
```

---

### Format 3: Person + Dollar Amount

**When:** Investment/funding stories with celebrity

**Structure:**
```
Primary: Celebrity/person photo (large, recognizable)
Overlay: Dollar amount (huge, bold)
Secondary: Company context (smaller text)
```

**Tools:** Canva

**Example (CORRECT):**
```
MrBeast photo (hero image)
"$200M" (large overlay)
"Investment from BitMine" (smaller)
"Crypto → Creator Economy" (context)

Why: MrBeast (300M) >> BitMine (unknown)
```

---

### Format 4: Logos + Number

**When:** Company moves, NO celebrity involved

**Structure:**
```
Top: Company logo(s)
Center: Large dollar amount
Bottom: Context
```

**Tools:** Canva

**Example:**
```
Stripe logo + Square logo
$500M
"Strategic Partnership"
```

---

### Format 5: Venn Diagram

**When:** Convergence of two industries/trends

**Structure:**
```
Circle 1: Industry A (icon/logo)
Circle 2: Industry B (icon/logo)
Overlap: The convergence (highlighted)
```

**Tools:** Canva, PowerPoint, Figma

**Example:**
```
Left: Crypto 🪙
Right: Creator Economy 🎥
Overlap: "$200M Equity Investment"
```

---

### Format 6: Timeline/Grid

**When:** Multiple examples, pattern recognition

**Structure:**
```
Row 1: Company | Action | Date
Row 2: Company | Action | Date
Row 3: Company | Action | Date
```

**Tools:** Canva table, Figma

**Example:**
```
Stripe → Agent Payments → June 2025
Square → AI APIs → Aug 2025
Chase → Agent Banking → Jan 2026
```

## Image Rights & Legal Safety

### Safe to Use (with attribution if required):

✅ **Company Press Kits**
- Explicitly for media use
- Check terms (usually require attribution)

✅ **LinkedIn Profile Photos**
- Fair use for commentary/news
- Discussing the person, not misrepresenting

✅ **Self-Created Screenshots**
- Transformative use for analysis
- You created the screenshot

✅ **Logos (Nominative Fair Use)**
- When discussing the company
- Not implying endorsement

✅ **Public Domain Images**
- Government photos (NASA, etc.)
- Expired copyrights

✅ **Creative Commons (Check License)**
- CC0 (fully public)
- CC BY (attribution required)
- Check specific license terms

### Require Permission or License:

⚠️ **Professional Photography**
- Contact photographer
- May require licensing fee

⚠️ **Stock Photos with Watermarks**
- Purchase license
- Remove watermark only after purchase

⚠️ **Other Creators' Original Graphics**
- Ask permission OR recreate

### Never Use:

❌ **Copyrighted Images Without License**
- Getty Images (unless paid)
- Shutterstock (unless paid)
- Professional photos with ©

❌ **Watermarked Stock Photos**
- Stealing, easily detected

❌ **Other People's Original Artwork**
- Infringement, unethical

**Attribution Example:**
```
Image: [Person Name] via [Company] Press Kit
Chart: Data from [Source], visualization by [You]
```

## Image Specs for LinkedIn

### Optimal Dimensions:

**Landscape (Link Preview):**
- 1200 x 628 px
- Aspect ratio: 1.91:1

**Square (Direct Upload):**
- 1200 x 1200 px
- Aspect ratio: 1:1
- OR 1080 x 1080 px

**Video:**
- 1920 x 1080 px (landscape)
- 1080 x 1080 px (square)
- Duration: 10-30 seconds ideal (max 10 min)
- Format: MP4, H.264 codec
- Subtitles: ALWAYS add (70% watch muted)

### File Requirements:

- **Format:** JPG (smaller file) or PNG (transparency)
- **Size:** <5MB (images), <200MB (video)
- **Resolution:** 72 DPI minimum (web)

### Text Overlay Requirements:

- **Must be readable on mobile**
- **Test at 25% size** - can you still read it?
- **High contrast** (white text on dark, or inverse)
- **Font size:** 48-72pt minimum for main text

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

✅ **Mobile Readable**
- [ ] Text legible at thumbnail size
- [ ] Key elements visible on small screen
- [ ] High contrast

✅ **Professional Quality**
- [ ] Not pixelated or blurry
- [ ] Properly aligned
- [ ] Consistent colors

✅ **Legally Safe**
- [ ] Images from legitimate sources
- [ ] No watermarked stock photos
- [ ] Fair use for logos (discussing company)
- [ ] Attribution where needed

✅ **Performance Optimized**
- [ ] Image/video size <5MB/<200MB
- [ ] Correct dimensions (1200x628 or 1200x1200)
- [ ] Video has subtitles (if applicable)
- [ ] First 2 seconds grab attention (video)

✅ **Enhances Hook**
- [ ] Visual reinforces message
- [ ] Shows what text describes
- [ ] Creates additional curiosity
- [ ] Stops the scroll

## Common Mistakes & Fixes

### ❌ Mistake: Using unknown company logo instead of celebrity

**Problem:** Post about MrBeast uses BitMine logo (nobody knows them)

**Fix:** Use MrBeast photo (300M followers, instant recognition)

**Rule:** Celebrity/recognizable > Unknown company

---

### ❌ Mistake: Text too small on mobile

**Problem:** Quote looks good on desktop, unreadable on phone

**Fix:** Test at 25% size, increase font to 48-72pt minimum

**Rule:** Mobile-first design

---

### ❌ Mistake: Low-res image from Google Images

**Problem:** Blurry, pixelated when uploaded

**Fix:** Filter for "Large" images, or use press kit version

**Rule:** Minimum 800x800px for faces, 1200x628 for posts

---

### ❌ Mistake: Using watermarked stock photo

**Problem:** Looks unprofessional, possibly illegal

**Fix:** Use Clearbit API, press kits, or self-created images

**Rule:** Never use watermarked images

---

### ❌ Mistake: Generic stock photo unrelated to content

**Problem:** Random office photo doesn't connect to story

**Fix:** Use person/company/product actually in the story

**Rule:** Visual must reinforce specific content

## Time-Saving Hacks

### 1. Create Reusable Templates

**In Canva:**
- Quote over face template
- Logos + dollar amount template
- Before/after comparison template
- Save with placeholder text
- Duplicate and customize each time

**Time saved:** 5-10 minutes per post

---

### 2. Build a Visual Library

**Create folders:**
```
/images/
  /people/        (headshots you'll reuse)
  /logos/         (company logos)
  /icons/         (reusable icons)
  /templates/     (Canva templates)
```

**Pre-save common logos:**
- Your company
- Partner companies
- Common mentions (Stripe, OpenAI, etc.)

**Time saved:** 3-5 minutes per post

---

### 3. Bookmark Quick Sources

**Browser bookmarks folder "Social Images":**
- Clearbit logo API base URL
- Your company press kit
- Unsplash (free photos)
- Canva templates dashboard
- Datawrapper

**Time saved:** 2-3 minutes per search

## LinkedIn Hook Patterns

### Pattern 1: Exclamatory Emotion Hooks

**Formula:** `[Emotion word]! [What happened]`

**Examples:**
- "That was quick! OpenAI has reportedly started rolling out a premium…" ([927 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_that-was-quick-openai-has-reportedly-started-activity-7022608835827699712-cPDH))
- "This is insane! Alibaba's new AI can bring static images to life, making…" ([442 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-is-insane-alibabas-new-ai-can-bring-activity-7168696645423882241-5Lb7))
- "This is wild! OpenAI's CTO doesn't know where the data they used to train…" ([611 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-is-wild-openais-cto-doesnt-know-where-activity-7174133575791886337-1Xti))

**Emotion Vocabulary:**
- "This is insane!"
- "This is wild!"
- "That was quick!"
- "This is a genius idea!"
- "This is absolutely brilliant!"

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Creates immediate emotional reaction
- ✅ Sets expectation for surprising content
- ✅ Simple, conversational language
- ✅ Exclamation point adds urgency

**Application for Simon:**
- ⚠️ Use sparingly - Simon's voice is more analytical than exclamatory
- ✅ Consider "Wild!" or "Fascinating!" for genuine surprise moments
- ✅ Adapt: "This is fascinating: [insight]" vs "This is wild!"

---

### Pattern 2: Superlative Promises

**Formula:** `[Superlative claim] [specific content type]`

**Examples:**
- "This is the best video you will see this year 😂" ([565 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-is-the-best-video-you-will-see-this-ugcPost-6997951424651223040-ET3K))
- "This lecture Steve Jobs gave in 1992 is a masterclass on conflict…" ([135 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-lecture-steve-jobs-gave-in-1992-is-a-activity-7137170098091417600-6srQ))

**Superlative Vocabulary:**
- "the best [X] you will see"
- "a masterclass on [topic]"
- "at its finest"

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Sets high expectation bar
- ✅ Creates FOMO (fear of missing out)
- ✅ Bold claim forces reader to validate

**Application for Simon:**
- ⚠️ Use rarely - can feel hyperbolic
- ✅ Only when you genuinely believe it's exceptional
- ✅ Adapt: "One of the clearest explanations of [X] I've seen"

---

### Pattern 3: Person-Focused Narrative Hook

**Formula:** `This is [Name]. [Context]. [Surprising outcome/action].`

**Examples:**
- "This is Oliver Daemen. In 2021, he became the youngest person ever to go…" ([249 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-is-oliver-daemen-in-2021-he-became-activity-7184627769014640641-MDl3))
- "This is absolutely brilliant 👏😂 From Forbes 40 under 40 to Prison Break…" ([246 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-is-absolutely-brilliant-from-forbes-activity-7002346551545061376-drMf))

**Structure:**
1. Introduce person with "This is [Name]"
2. Provide context (year, role, situation)
3. Reveal surprising twist or achievement

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Immediate human connection
- ✅ Creates curiosity about the person
- ✅ Story-driven (we're wired for narratives)
- ✅ Specific names = concrete, not abstract

**Application for Simon:**
- ✅ Perfect fit for Simon's analytical, specific style
- ✅ Use when profiling founders, executives, or companies
- ✅ Add Simon's analytical layer: "This is [Name]. [Context]. But here's what's interesting..."

---

### Pattern 4: Strategic Emoji Use

**LinkedIn Pattern:** Emojis for emphasis, often at end of hook

**Examples:**
- "This work hack is social engineering at its finest 👏🏼😂" ([644 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-work-hack-is-social-engineering-at-its-activity-6929100178301857792-hJZm))
- "This is the best video you will see this year 😂"
- "This is a genius idea! Well done Sweden🇸🇪" ([95 comments](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/linasbeliunas_this-is-a-genius-idea-well-done-sweden-activity-6918271473975488512-hSfe))

**Emoji Vocabulary:**
- 😂 (laughing) - for humorous/absurd content
- 👏🏼 (clapping) - for impressive achievements
- 😳 (shocked face) - for surprising revelations
- 🇸🇪 (country flags) - for geographic context

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Adds visual interest in text-heavy feed
- ✅ Reinforces emotional tone
- ✅ Makes content feel approachable

**Application for Simon:**
- ⚠️ Simon prefers minimal emoji use (per voice patterns)
- ✅ If used, 1 emoji maximum
- ✅ Only when it genuinely adds clarity (e.g., 😳 for "wild" moments)
- ❌ Avoid generic emoji stuffing

## Newsletter/Substack Hook Patterns

### Pattern 5: Emoji-Punctuated Headlines

**Formula:** `[Company/topic]'s [action verb] to [outcome] [relevant emojis]`

**Examples:**
- "Sony's stablecoin gambit to reshape entertainment payments 🪙🎮" ([Source: Linas's Newsletter](https://linas.substack.com/p/weeklyfintechpulse379))
- "Revolut CEO wants to revolutionize Venture Capital with $250M AI-Powered Fund 🤖💸" ([Source: Linas's Newsletter](https://linas.substack.com/p/weeklyfintechpulse351))
- "JPMorgan claims its first major Open Banking victim: payments giant Visa 😳🏦"
- "Top FinTech & AI Stories of 2025: A Year of Convergence 🔗⚡" ([Source: Linas's Newsletter](https://linas.substack.com/p/topfintechstories2025))

**Action Verbs:**
- "gambit to reshape"
- "wants to revolutionize"
- "claims its first victim"
- "unlocks"
- "unveils"

**Emoji Placement:**
- Always at END of headline
- Usually 2 emojis (topic + outcome)
- Examples: 🪙🎮 (stablecoin + gaming), 🤖💸 (AI + money)

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Institutional credibility (big company names)
- ✅ Action verbs create movement/urgency
- ✅ Emojis provide visual scanning landmarks
- ✅ Specific outcomes (not vague "disruption")

**Application for Simon:**
- ✅ Strong fit - combines specificity + analytical framing
- ✅ Use company names + specific dollar amounts
- ⚠️ Adapt emoji use to Simon's preference (0-1 max)
- ✅ Action verbs: "reshaping", "redefining", "shifting"

---

### Pattern 6: Stakes-First Framing

**Formula:** Frame as significant market shift or "moment"

**Examples:**
- "FinTech's Maturation Moment: how AI and sustainable growth are reshaping financial services 🤖📈"
- "The Day the AI Builders Felt Behind 🧠⚡" ([Source: Linas's Newsletter](https://linas.substack.com/p/aibuildersfeltbehind))
- "A Year of Convergence" (2025 recap)

**Framing Words:**
- "Moment"
- "The Day"
- "Era"
- "Shift"
- "Convergence"

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Positions content as historically significant
- ✅ Creates urgency (don't miss this moment)
- ✅ Appeals to forward-thinking audience
- ✅ Makes news feel like narrative

**Application for Simon:**
- ✅ Perfect for Simon's "what does this mean?" analytical style
- ✅ Frames observation as pattern recognition
- ✅ Example: "Maybe crypto is growing up. Maybe this is the moment it stops trying to reinvent creators and just... invests in them."

**Why it works:**
- ✅ Concrete > abstract
- ✅ Numbers make claims verifiable
- ✅ Shows scale/stakes
- ✅ Attracts finance-oriented audience

**Application for Simon:**
- ✅ 100% aligned with Simon's analytical, specific style
- ✅ Already using this (e.g., "$200M in MrBeast's company")

Each day I focus on 3 impactful stories at the intersection of Finance & Technology.
Coupled with things worth watching & most important money movements,

## Key Takeaways for Best Practices

### ✅ DO Adopt:

1. **Person-focused narrative hooks**
   - "This is [Name]. [Context]. [Surprising thing]."
   - Fits Simon's specific, analytical style

2. **Dollar amount specificity**
   - Always include specific figures when available
   - Makes content concrete and verifiable

3. **Action verbs with stakes**
   - "gambit to reshape", "claims its victim", "unlocks"
   - Shows movement, not static observation

4. **Stakes-first framing**
   - Position observations as significant moments
   - "Maybe this is crypto growing up" = framing as moment

5. **Institutional credibility**
   - Company names, tickers, specific roles
   - Already doing this - keep it up

6. **Structured sections**
   - Make analysis structure more explicit
   - Help readers scan for what they want

### ⚠️ Adapt for Simon:

1. **Emotional hooks**
   - Linas: "This is insane!"
   - Simon: "Wild!" or "Fascinating!" (rarely, when genuine)

2. **Emoji use**
   - Linas: 2-4 per post
   - Simon: 0-1 max, only when it adds clarity

3. **Superlative promises**
   - Linas: "the best video you will see"
   - Simon: "One of the clearest explanations I've seen"

### ❌ DON'T Copy:

1. **Generic excitement**
   - Linas can do "This is insane!" for brand
   - Simon's brand is thoughtful analysis

2. **Certainty where there's uncertainty**
   - Keep "I'm stumped" and "Maybe it's..."
   - Simon's honesty > Linas's exclamation points

3. **Over-optimization at expense of voice**
   - Performance < Authenticity + Clarity
   - Simon's analytical uncertainty is a feature, not a bug

## Hook Formula Recommendations for Simon

### Formula 1: Person-Focused Analytical Hook
```
This is [Name]. [Role/Context].

[Surprising action/outcome].

But here's what's interesting: [analytical observation]
```

**Example:**
```
This is Oliver Daemen. In 2021, he became the youngest person to go to space.

Now he's investing in [X].

But here's what's interesting: [pattern Simon notices]
```

### Formula 2: Stakes-First with Dollar Specificity
```
[Company] just [action verb] [specific $ amount] into [what].

Maybe this is about [bigger pattern].

Or it's [alternative interpretation].
```

**Example:**
```
BitMine just invested $200M into MrBeast's company.

Maybe this is about crypto growing up.

Or it's 2026 weirdness.
```

### Formula 3: Pattern Recognition with Humility
```
[Observation about trend].

I've been thinking about [question].

Maybe it's [speculation]. Maybe it's [alternative].
```

**Example:**
```
Crypto has been trying to do creators forever.

What if the answer wasn't tokens or NFTs?

Maybe the answer all along was to just... invest in them.
```

# Algorithm Research Prompt Template

Use this template quarterly to research any social platform's algorithm. Replace `[PLATFORM]` with LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, etc.

---

## The Prompt

```
Analyze [PLATFORM]'s current algorithm priorities and provide a detailed breakdown of:

### Algorithm Factors
- Primary ranking signals and their weight/importance
- Content format preferences (video length, image specs, text limits)
- Engagement velocity requirements for viral potential
- Optimal posting times and frequency

### Optimization Strategy
- 5 specific tactics to maximize algorithmic reach
- Content structure recommendations
- Hashtag/keyword strategies
- Cross-platform amplification methods

### Success Metrics
- KPIs to track for algorithm optimization
- Benchmarks for viral threshold on this platform
- Timeline expectations for results

Base recommendations on current [YEAR] platform data and provide specific, actionable steps I can implement immediately.
```

## Tips for Better Research

**1. Search for primary sources:**
- Official platform blogs
- Platform creator newsletters
- Studies from Buffer, Hootsuite, Sprout Social
- Industry analyses from trusted sources

**2. Look for data, not opinions:**
- ❌ "I think carousels work better"
- ✅ "Study of 10K posts shows carousels get 1.5x engagement"

**3. Find recent insights:**
- Focus on last 3-6 months
- Algorithms change quickly
- Older advice may be outdated

**4. Cross-reference multiple sources:**
- One study might be an outlier
- Look for consistent patterns across sources

**5. Test on your own content:**
- Generic trends might not apply to your audience
- Always validate with your own data

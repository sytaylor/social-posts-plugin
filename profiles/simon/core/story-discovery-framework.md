## Story Evaluation Criteria

### The 3-Filter System

Every potential story must pass through these filters:

#### Filter 1: The Simon Filter
**Does this story enable Simon's voice?**

✅ **YES if:**
- Allows for genuine questions ("I'm stumped" moments)
- Has both-sides interpretations ("Maybe X. Or it's Y.")
- Contains specific details (company structure, dollar amounts, tickers)
- Infrastructure/rails angle (not just consumer surface)
- Second-order effects to analyze

❌ **NO if:**
- Requires hot take without nuance
- Only surface-level consumer story
- No analytical depth available
- Forces certainty where there's uncertainty

**Example:**
```
✅ "BitMine (BMNR) invested $200M in MrBeast"
   → Enables: company structure analysis, crypto maturation thesis, creator economy convergence

❌ "New crypto token launches"
   → No depth, requires hype, doesn't enable analytical voice
```

---

#### Filter 2: The Audience Filter
**Will Simon's audience care?**

**Simon's Audience:**
- FinTech founders & operators
- VCs investing in financial services
- Tech executives at banks
- Product managers in payments
- Financial infrastructure geeks

✅ **YES if story has:**
- **Infrastructure implications** - "What does this mean for the rails?"
- **Business model innovation** - New way to monetize/scale
- **Market structure shifts** - "This changes how X works"
- **Second-order effects** - "If this works, then..."
- **Money mechanics** - How capital flows, who wins/loses

❌ **NO if story is:**
- Pure consumer feature launch (no infrastructure angle)
- Celebrity news without financial implications
- Generic "AI will change everything" hype
- Too niche (only relevant to 100 people)
- Too broad (everyone is covering it the same way)

**Test:** Would a FinTech product manager send this to their team Slack?

---

#### Filter 3: The Performance Filter
**Will this perform on LinkedIn?**

**High-Probability Performers:**
- ✅ Specific dollar amounts ($200M, $1.2B fundraises)
- ✅ Name-brand companies (Google, Stripe, Coinbase)
- ✅ Unexpected combinations (crypto + MrBeast, banks + AI agents)
- ✅ "Finally" moments (industry maturation, thesis validation)
- ✅ Person-focused stories (founder profiles, unexpected moves)
- ✅ Contrarian angles on popular news

**Low-Probability Performers:**
- ❌ Inside baseball (too niche, requires context)
- ❌ Old news (>48h unless "Finally" angle)
- ❌ No visual potential (can't show the difference)
- ❌ Requires 10 tweets of context to understand
- ❌ Pure opinion with no news hook

**Performance Indicators:**
- Can you create a side-by-side visual?
- Does it have a "wait, what?" moment?
- Is there a person/company to profile?
- Does it validate or challenge a thesis?

## Story Prioritization Matrix

### Priority 1: PUBLISH TODAY
**Criteria:** Passes all 3 filters + timely

**Characteristics:**
- Breaking news with analytical angle
- "Finally" moment that validates your thesis
- Unexpected combo (crypto + creator, bank + AI)
- Specific dollar amounts + company details
- Strong visual potential

**Example:**
```
✅ "Google drops Personal Intelligence AI (no one can replicate)"
   → Simon Filter: Yes (AI maturation, infrastructure)
   → Audience: Yes (FinTech leaders care about AI agents)
   → Performance: Yes (name brand, "Finally" angle, visual comparison)
   → PUBLISH: Today
```

---

### Priority 2: DEVELOP THIS WEEK
**Criteria:** Passes 2/3 filters, needs development

**Characteristics:**
- Good story, but need to find unique angle
- Requires more research for specifics
- Part of emerging pattern (wait for 3rd example)
- Could be "Finally" moment with context

**Development Actions:**
- Research company structure, dollar amounts
- Find person to profile
- Create visual concept
- Connect to broader pattern

**Example:**
```
⚠️ "New crypto fund launches"
   → Needs: Who's behind it? Dollar amount? What's different?
   → If it's "finally" a TradFi + crypto convergence → Priority 1
   → If it's just another fund → Skip
```

---

### Priority 3: WATCHLIST
**Criteria:** Interesting, but not ready yet

**Characteristics:**
- First or second signal of pattern (wait for 3rd)
- Intriguing person/company to watch
- Thesis developing (not validated yet)
- Too early (seed round, needs traction proof)

**Watchlist Actions:**
- Add to "Things I'm Watching" doc
- Set Google Alert for company/person
- Note in calendar to check in 30/60/90 days

---

### Priority 4: SKIP
**Criteria:** Fails 2+ filters

**Characteristics:**
- Pure hype, no substance
- Too niche or too broad
- No Simon angle
- Old news, no new angle
- Generic consumer feature

**Time saved:** Don't force posts on stories that don't fit

## Story-to-Hook Mapping

Once you've prioritized a story, match it to the right hook pattern:

### If Story Has: → Use Hook Pattern:

**Big company + specific $ amount**
→ Pattern 11 (Dollar Amount Specificity)
→ "Google invested $X into Y"

**Unexpected person move**
→ Pattern 8 (Person-Focused Narrative)
→ "This is [Name]. [Context]. [Surprise]."

**Industry maturation signal**
→ Pattern 13 (The "Finally" Moment)
→ "[Topic] is finally [verb]: [Evidence]"

**Strategic company move**
→ Pattern 12 (Action Verbs with Stakes)
→ "[Company]'s gambit to reshape [X]"

**Convergence of two worlds**
→ Pattern 9 (Stakes-First Framing)
→ "[Event]. Maybe this is about [bigger pattern]."

**Genuinely surprising news**
→ Pattern 10 (Exclamatory Emotion Hook)
→ "Wild! [Specific surprise]"

**Pattern you've been tracking**
→ Pattern 3 (Contrarian Hook) or Pattern 13 (Finally)
→ "Everyone says X. They're wrong." OR "Finally..."

## Daily Workflow

### Morning Routine (15-20 minutes)

**7:00 AM - News Scan:**
1. Check Tier 1 sources (TechCrunch, The Block, etc.)
2. Scan LinkedIn feed for trending topics
3. Check newsletter inbox (Linas, Stratechery, etc.)

**7:15 AM - Filter & Prioritize:**
1. Apply 3-Filter System to interesting stories
2. Identify 1-2 Priority 1 stories (publish today)
3. Add 2-3 to Priority 2 (develop this week)
4. Note any Watchlist items

**7:20 AM - Decision:**
- If Priority 1 story exists → Move to Hook Selection
- If no Priority 1 → Check Priority 2 backlog
- If nothing strong → Skip posting today (quality > consistency)

---

**Step 1: Story Type → Hook Pattern**
Use Story-to-Hook Mapping above

**Step 2: Simon Voice Check**
- Does this hook feel authentic?
- Can you genuinely say "I'm stumped" or "Maybe X. Or Y."?
- Are you forcing excitement or is surprise genuine?

**Step 3: Performance Check**
- Hook in first 210 characters?
- Specific dollar amounts?
- Company names + context?
- Clear curiosity gap?

## Pattern Recognition System

### The "3 Examples = Trend" Rule

**How it works:**
- First example: Note in Watchlist
- Second example: "Interesting pattern forming"
- Third example: **Time to post**

**Example:**
```
Example 1: Stripe launches agent payments (June 2025)
→ Watchlist: "Banks enabling AI agents?"

Example 2: Square adds AI agent APIs (August 2025)
→ Priority 2: "Pattern forming - research more"

Example 3: Chase announces agent banking (January 2026)
→ Priority 1: "Banks are FINALLY building for AI agents" post
```

**Post structure:**
```
Banks are finally building for AI agents.

Stripe → Agent payments (June)
Square → AI agent APIs (Aug)
Chase → Agent banking (Jan)

Maybe this is about AI reaching real-world commerce.
Or it's the infrastructure finally catching up to the hype.
```

**Story:** Small fintech adds AI chatbot to app

### Example 3: Interesting Founder, Too Early

**Story:** Ex-Stripe engineer starts new payments company, raises $2M seed

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Pitfall 1: Chasing Every Trend
**Problem:** Posting on every viral topic
**Why it fails:** Loses your unique voice, becomes noise
**Solution:** Only post if you have unique Simon angle

### ❌ Pitfall 2: Too Inside Baseball
**Problem:** Stories only 100 people care about
**Why it fails:** No engagement, wastes strong hook
**Solution:** Apply Audience Filter rigorously

### ❌ Pitfall 3: Too Late
**Problem:** Posting 3 days after everyone else
**Why it fails:** Unless you have "Finally" angle, you're noise
**Solution:** Post within 24-48h OR wait for pattern (3 examples)

### ❌ Pitfall 4: Forcing Posts
**Problem:** "I should post today" → posts weak story
**Why it fails:** Trains audience to expect low quality
**Solution:** Quality > consistency. Skip days when no Priority 1 story

### ❌ Pitfall 5: No Visual Plan
**Problem:** Great hook, but no image/visual
**Why it fails:** Text-only posts underperform
**Solution:** Don't commit to post until visual strategy exists (next section)

## Success Metrics

Track these to refine your story selection:

### Post Performance:
- Engagement rate (reactions + comments per 1000 followers)
- Comment quality (real discussion vs "great post!")
- Reposts/shares (signal of value)
- Profile visits (are you attracting right audience?)

### Pattern Recognition:
- "Finally" posts (did your thesis validate?)
- Contrarian takes (were you right to go against consensus?)
- Person-focused posts (are you good at spotting talent?)

### Efficiency:
- Time from story discovery → publish
- Hit rate (% of Priority 1 stories that perform)
- False positives (stories you thought would hit, didn't)
